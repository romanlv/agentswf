usage: awf wake <run> [--timer <name>] [--cwd <directory>] [--run-root <directory>] [--json]

Wakes a parked run of a workflow with events now, rather than at its wait's timer: once what
parked it is fixed, its retry need not wait out its time. The timer the run's wait takes fires
now, recorded as the operator's wake in its in.jsonl, its own due kept; every other timer stays
as it was. A wait with several names one with --timer. A run at a wait its process holds live,
as keepAlive does, is told at once. A wait with no timer, a run that is neither parked nor at a
wait, or one that is stuck, isn't woken: awf runs <run> says what wakes it.

Prints the timer it fired and what was done to the run: continued, nudged, or why not. --json
prints one document, or a refusal whose code says why it failed: usage, wake, no-run, ambiguous,
id, unreachable, no-engine, other-engine or internal-error. Exits 0 when the timer fired; 1 when
the run isn't found or can't be read, or its attempt could not be started (the wake is kept, and
awf tick tries again); 2 on a usage error, or when the run has no timer to fire: one without events, finished, stopped
or stuck, or at a wait with none.
